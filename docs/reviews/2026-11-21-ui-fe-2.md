## Defects, ranked by what a judge notices in two minutes

1. **Three totals for the same question.** `/tablero`: "158 personas alcanzadas" in the app, "126 Personas impactadas" in the embedded Metabase 600 px lower; `/informe/global/exportar`: "entre 118 y 131". Shots: `tipc2-1440-llegada.png`, `tipc2-1440-masa-elegidos.png`, `c3070-1440-global-exportar-completa.png`. The app total counts hair donors (Melenas 73 = 55 donors + 18 beneficiaries), which the report says are not impacted. Fix: the Tablero headline must be "personas impactadas" with the same rule as the Global report (donors apart, sum labelled as a sum); until Metabase reads the same data, hide its four number cards and keep only the charts.
2. **Metabase does not follow the filters.** Programa: only the first value is applied (app 158 with both programs, Metabase stays on "Melenas en Movimiento", 20). Municipio, Mes and Servicio show as chips in Metabase but its person count stays 126 (app: 26, 53, 40). Shots: `tipc2-1440-filtro-programa-metabase.png`, `tipc2-1440-filtro-ciudad-metabase.png`, `tipc2-390-filtro-combinado-metabase.png`. The page says so honestly ("hoy solo aplica el filtro Programa"), but the caption under the frame still claims all four are received. Fix: wire multi-value params, or remove the caption and disable the frame while a non-program filter is active.
3. **Bulk action does nothing visible.** `/tablero`, table "Posibles duplicados por decidir". Selecting 2 pairs and "Son la misma persona" wrote 2 entries to `eira:decisiones` (`tipo persona par par-01/02 decision misma`), verified on a8f45b6 and c2ef447. The two rows disappear only in browser state; the band still says "3 pares", the total stays 158, and after reload all 3 rows are back. Shots: `tipc2-1440-masa-elegidos.png`, `tipc2-1440-masa-aplicado.png`. With the engine this may resolve; without it the demo shows an action with no effect. Fix: read pending pairs minus the decisions already in the stream, and update the band from the same list.
4. **"¿Es la misma persona?" still cannot be decided.** `/informe/tamizaje-rosa/calidad`, `c3070-1440-tamizaje-rosa-calidad-completa.png`. It is now a field table, but "Identificación" is one string, identical on both sides ("M. E. L. · Sayula · 42 años") under the reason "Mismo ID, nombres distintos"; the only shaded cells are file and row. The bulk table has the same problem. Fix: separate rows for nombre, edad, ciudad, and show the part of the name that differs.
5. **Phone header cuts the main button.** 390 px, every route: "Subir archivos" ends at x=446 of 390, shown as "Subir arc" (`tipc2-390-llegada.png`). Fix: icon-only or drop the label under 480 px.
6. **Filter values are raw ids.** Programa options and chips read "melenas-en-movimiento", "tamizaje-rosa" (`tipc2-1440-filtro-programa-multi.png`); Periodo was fixed to "abr 2026". The Global row text also says "personas en melenas-en-movimiento y tamizaje-rosa". Fix: catalogue names.
7. **Exportar has no primary action.** "Descargar CSV" outline pill, "Imprimir o guardar PDF" smaller with square corners; the report card is about 100 px narrower than the footer rule (`c3070-1440-global-exportar-completa.png`). Unchanged since review 1.
8. **Stepper lock text collides.** `/informe/<alcance>/subir` and `/calidad` at 1440: "Primero revisa la calidad de los d…" truncated and touching the next step (`c3070-1440-tamizaje-rosa-subir-completa.png`). Fix: one line under the stepper.
9. **"Cómo se calculó" says nothing.** Every figure: "Conteo exacto leído de la base de datos del proyecto… La consulta tardó 17 ms." No rule, no source, no cut-off (`tipc2-1440-como-se-calculo.png`). Ian asked for plain Spanish that explains the count. Fix: one sentence per figure ("Personas distintas con ID en los archivos X e Y, al 30/09/2026; quien está en dos programas cuenta una vez").

### Inconsistencies to flag
- Unit drift: women (7,800 → 13,000 → 14,000 → 16,275 → 40,000) vs persons (2,000 in 2022; 51,000 in 2026) vs studies (6,472 target; 64,023; 86,000). Persons ≠ women ≠ studies; the guide itself warns 51,000 is not unique patients.
- Jump 16,275 women (undated) → 40,000 women (2025) is large; plausibly includes the menstrual programme (6,606 women in 2025) and school participations, but this is an inference, not stated.
- 86,000 studies / 40,000 women ≈ 2.15 studies per woman (arithmetic, not published).
- 51,000 "personas" (Sept 2026) vs 40,000 "mujeres" (2025): different unit and cut-off; no breakdown published.
- Comunidades de Origen 1,400 vs 1,443 in the same release; and it is a target, not an outcome.
- CUSur article: dateline 8 Oct 2024 but says "martes 9 de octubre" and images from 2025-11.
- Financial statements: all spending classified as "servicios de apoyo", "Servicios por programa" = 0; cash-flow sheet shows "#REF!" errors (Excel origin); "Donativos recibidos" line equals cash only while total includes in-kind.
- Founded 2017 (site, guide, LinkedIn) vs SAT RFC EMR1909051SA, which suggests legal incorporation 2019-09-05 (inference from RFC format).
- Contact email: site shows contacto@eira.org, guide shows contacto@eiraac.org. WhatsApp numbers differ between pages (3411563921 on home link, 3411596783 on hacemos.html, 3411596782 in guide).

## C. What donors seem to ask for (from public material)
- Corporate allies (BerryMex, Driscoll's) publish: women benefited, studies by type (ultrasonido, mastografía, Papanicolaou, nutrición), communities/municipalities, states, campaign period, supplies delivered (pads/packs). 
- Donataria reporting (SAT/CEMEFI): Estado de Actividades, Situación Financiera, Cambios en Patrimonio, Flujos de Efectivo, Cédula de aplicación y destino de donativos, Notas, "informe anual de actividades" with evidence of publication.
- Guide's Reto 3 asks for: indicators with fecha de corte, denominador y fuente; separation of personas únicas / servicios / atenciones / estudios / entregas de insumos / participaciones; filters by programa, periodo, municipio, tipo de servicio; trazabilidad de ajustes manuales.
- No public grant report from Driscoll's, Berrymex, SVCF, Teletón, Fundación Merced or Nacional Monte de Piedad was found.
